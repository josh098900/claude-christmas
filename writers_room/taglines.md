# Tagline candidates

The end card holds the tagline and **Claude** in clean type, plus the fan-made disclaimer. I tested each candidate against the finished film with two questions:

1. Does it say what the film *showed*: Claude helped her think, and people did the doing?
2. Does it land the moment after the Earth shot without explaining the film?

| # | Candidate | Fits the story | Fits the brand | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Keep thinking.** | ●●○ | ●●● | Anthropic's real line. Elegant, but it rewards the *thinking* and forgets the street. |
| 2 | **Some problems need a whole street.** | ●●● | ●●○ | Echoes the Turn ("This bit needs more people") and the climax. Generous to humans, and quietly honest about what an AI can't do. |
| 3 | Think it through. Then knock on doors. | ●●● | ●●● | Both halves of the film in eight words. A little instructional. |
| 4 | It takes a street. | ●●○ | ●○○ | Too close to "it takes a village". |
| 5 | A space to think. A street to help. | ●●○ | ●●● | Nods to "a space to think" (ad-free), but it's clever rather than moving. |
| 6 | Help with the thinking. The rest is people. | ●●● | ●●○ | True but flat. |
| 7 | Bright ideas need good people. | ●●○ | ●○○ | Bulb pun, so it's cute and slight. |
| 8 | The best ideas bring people together. | ●●○ | ●●○ | Generic. Could close any ad. |
| 9 | Seen from space. Started at a bedroom desk. | ●●○ | ●○○ | Nice scale contrast, but it puts the desk (and the tablet) at the centre. |
| 10 | Think big. Ask around. | ●●○ | ●●○ | Snappy, but it loses the tenderness. |
| 11 | For the ideas that need everyone. | ●●○ | ●●○ | Soft, and a bit vague. |
| 12 | Good ideas need good neighbours. | ●●● | ●○○ | Warm, but sounds like a council campaign. |

## Shortlist

**2** and **3**, with **1** as the benchmark.

## Decision

**"Some problems need a whole street."** It is the line the whole film argues for. After a shot where one tiny dot is the brightest thing on Earth, it tells you *why* the dot is bright: not because of a tablet, but because a street turned up.

**"Keep thinking."** is Anthropic's own line and the one to beat. It would sit better on a campaign card than on this story.

This decision was confirmed by the fresh-eyes critics in Phase 6; see DECISIONS.md.
